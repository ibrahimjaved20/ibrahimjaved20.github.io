Privacy &
analytics.

I use PostHog to understand which projects people explore and whether this portfolio helps them find what they need.

What is measured

Page visits, referral sources, device and browser details, approximate location, sections viewed, scroll depth, link clicks, résumé downloads and approximate active time. A browser identifier connects visits so I can understand returning visitors. Providing an optional email can link those visits to your details.

What stays private

Chat questions and assistant answers are recorded in PostHog so Ibrahim can review conversations and improve the assistant. If you optionally share your name or email in the chat, those details are recorded too. An email links activity from this browser to that identity; it is not independently verified. Visitors who skip these fields remain unidentified. Session replay records page views, scrolling, pointer movements and clicks so Ibrahim can understand how the portfolio is used. Optional name, email, typed chat input and displayed chat messages are visible in visual recordings. Password fields remain masked. Click events include device category, viewport size and interaction type (such as mouse, touch or keyboard) to compare phone and computer use. Chat transcripts and any voluntarily supplied contact details are collected separately as described above. Console log recording and network body/header capture are disabled in this website configuration. Avoid confidential information.

The AI representative

If you ask the representative a question, that message and recent conversation context are sent to my Cloudflare Worker and its hosted Qwen model to produce an answer. This is separate from analytics. Please avoid confidential information.

Your choice

You can turn off analytics for this browser below. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals are also respected. Blocking analytics does not affect the portfolio or chatbot.

Questions?

Email ibrahimjaved20@outlook.com. Analytics data is processed by PostHog in its US Cloud region. PostHog privacy information.
